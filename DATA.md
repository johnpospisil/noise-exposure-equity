## IPUMS NHGIS: licence and citation

This project uses IPUMS NHGIS geographic crosswalks under the IPUMS NHGIS usage licence (accepted 2026-11-17):

- **No redistribution.** NHGIS files are not included in this repository, its releases or its app. To reproduce the analysis, create a free account at <https://www.nhgis.org> and download the crosswalks yourself (instructions are added in P04).
- **Citation.** Cite NHGIS in the form given at <https://www.nhgis.org/citation-and-use> (exact text to be pasted here in P02, with the NHGIS version used).
- **Bibliography.** After publication, submit the article to the IPUMS NHGIS bibliography.

Published outputs from this project are aggregate statistics computed by the project, not NHGIS data.
