How loud is my neighbourhood?

Enter an address in one of the 20 largest US metro areas. You'll see how many of your neighbourhood's residents live with modelled transportation noise of 55 dBA or more. That is roughly the level of a busy street, averaged over the whole day and night.

Your address goes only to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder, to find your census block group. This page stores nothing.

Read this first

  • Modelled, not measured. These are estimates from the U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics' National Transportation Noise Map (2022). Nobody measured the noise at your door.
  • Your neighbourhood, not your home. Results describe the census block group (about 1,500 people) your address falls in. BTS says its map should not be used to judge noise at individual locations.
  • Not all noise. Road means major roads only. Rail does not include subways. Neighbours, construction and nightlife are not included.

Sources: BTS National Transportation Noise Map (2022); U.S. Census Bureau (2020 Census; ACS 2018–22); address matching by the Census Geocoder. Read the full analysis.
